How to use the pages

  • Guided tour walks through the topic step by step. Press Space to play or pause, ← → to step; the speed selector sets how fast it runs. Sliders the tour controls are locked and marked.
  • Explore unlocks every parameter. Numbers update live; the panel shows the derived quantities.
  • Below each simulation: the key equations, exercises with hidden answers, the model's assumptions and the references for every number used.

The simulations are teaching models: every constant is taken from a cited source and the limitations are listed on each page. They reproduce trends and typical magnitudes well, but they do not replace calibrated process data from a real tool.

Works on desktop and mobile, in light and dark mode. No data is collected.
