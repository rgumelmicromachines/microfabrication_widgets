Microfabrication: interactive lessons

Each lesson pairs a figure that reacts to its parameters with a tour of questions and tasks, worked exercises, and the sources and limits of its model.

4.2Ion implantation

Range and straggle, dose and peak concentration, junction depth, masking thickness and beam time.

5.1Thermal oxidation

Deal–Grove model: dry vs wet oxidation, temperature, orientation, time to a target thickness and the colour of the oxide.

5.2CVD growth rate

Grove model: when does the surface reaction limit growth, and when the gas flow? Boundary layer, temperature and the regime boundary.

5.3Evaporation & sputtering

Line-of-sight deposition: thickness uniformity, mean free path versus pressure, shadowing and lift-off.

5.4Lift-off

Deposition into a resist opening: undercut and positive profiles, sidewall coverage in evaporation and sputtering, shift of the line at the wafer edge.

5.5Film stress & wafer bow

Intrinsic and thermal-mismatch stress, the Stoney equation, wafer curvature and how stress is measured.

6.1Etching silicon

Anisotropic KOH/TMAH with 54.74° walls, isotropic etching, Bosch DRIE; mask selectivity and undercut.

6.2Dry etching

Reactive ion etching: ions and radicals, anisotropy and bias, overetch, selectivity and end-point detection by laser interferometry.

7.1Electromigration

Black’s equation: from a stress test to a ten-year lifetime. Acceleration factor, failure statistics, aluminium vs copper and the Blech length.

7.2Stress migration

Voids without current: thermal stress against diffusion, the temperature of fastest voiding and why a hotter storage test is not a faster one.

8.1Four-point probe

Sheet resistance, correction factors for small and thick samples, van der Pauw, resistivity and doping.

8.2Film colour & reflectometry

Thin-film interference: why oxide and nitride films are coloured, the colour chart, viewing angle, antireflection coatings and thickness from the spectrum.

9.1Lithography run

A game with a score: take a wafer from the AZ 1500 data sheet through coating, alignment, exposure, development and etch to the electrical test of an aluminium resistor.

Using a lesson

  • Explore is where a lesson opens: every slider is yours, and the numbers update live in the characteristics table next to the figure.
  • Challenges, the second tab, takes you through the topic in numbered steps. Some ask you to predict what happens and then show it on the figure; others set a task that you solve with the sliders, with Show me if you get stuck. Nothing is locked, the steps can be taken in any order, and they give points. Adding #tour to a lesson’s address opens it there.
  • Enlarge figure under the figure shows it alone on the screen, for a projector or a phone held sideways.
  • Below each simulation: the key equations, exercises with hidden answers, the model's assumptions and the references for every number used.

What the models are

The simulations are teaching models: every constant is taken from a cited source and the limitations are listed on each page. They reproduce trends and typical magnitudes well, but they do not replace calibrated process data from a real tool.

Works on desktop and mobile, in light and dark mode. No data is collected.
